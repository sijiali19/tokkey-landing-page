Run a 35B model
on a 16GB Mac

A step-by-step guide, from picking the model to your first answer, with the speeds we measured.

Tokkey team
On this page
  1. What you need
  2. Pick the model
  3. What we measured

A 16 GB Mac can run small and mid-size models well. Bigger ones, like the 35B class, usually don’t fit in memory. This guide shows how to run one with Tokkey attached, and what to expect.

What you need

  • A Mac with Apple silicon and 16 GB of memory
  • Tokkey, plugged in and connected
  • The Tokkey app

Pick the model

Open the Models page. With Tokkey attached, Qwen 3.6 35B-A3B shows as “Opens up with Tokkey”. Download the recommended size.

Terminal
$ tokkey run qwen3.6:35b-a3b
